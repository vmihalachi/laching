# Plans (archive)

The design and sequence briefs of three released versions, kept as the record of why things are as they are:

- `0.5/`: the companion layer (`LachingCompanion`, `LachingCompanionUI`, `Laching.Companion`, `Laching.Testing`,
  `tools/`).
- `0.7/`: the Mac companion shell (what the apps repeated on top of 0.6).
- `0.8/`: the Windows companion shell (`Laching.Companion.Shell`, `Laching.Windows.Companion`). *The map* in
  `0.8/README.md` is still how the C# shell mirrors the Swift one.

Nothing new goes here. Planned work is in [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/vmihalachi/laching/issues): what 0.8 left
unchecked or undecided became #6–#13.
